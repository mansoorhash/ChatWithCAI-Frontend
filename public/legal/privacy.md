## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
### Personal information you disclose to us
_**In Short:** We collect personal information that you provide to us._

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us.

**Personal Information Provided by You.** The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. The personal information we collect may include the following:
* Account information, such as your name, username, email address, and confirmation that you meet the Services' age requirement, where provided;
* Authentication credentials, such as your password, which is processed through our authentication service provider;
* Conversation content, including prompts, messages, AI-generated responses, and chat history;
* Preferences and feedback, including model-related settings, ratings, training-consent choices, and other feedback you provide; and
* Derived conversation information, including generated chat titles, conversation summaries, and long-term facts or context automatically identified from conversations; and
* Service and routing information, including conversation identifiers, selected models, model availability, usage counts, training-choice records, and policy-acceptance records, including their timestamps and applicable versions.
* Information you provide when contacting us for customer support, privacy requests, or other service-related matters.
* Email delivery information, including recipient addresses, message identifiers, subject lines, delivery status, bounce and complaint information, and suppression status.

**Sensitive Information.** We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information. However, because CAI accepts free-form prompts, you may choose to submit information that is considered sensitive under applicable law. If you do, we will process that information as described in this Privacy Policy to provide the Services. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you have the right to disclose it.

You are responsible for ensuring that the account and contact information you provide is accurate and kept up to date.

### Information automatically collected
_**In Short:** Some information — such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address and/or browser and device characteristics — is collected automatically when you visit our Services._

We automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you visit or use the Services. Through Cloudflare and our systems hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), this information may include your IP address, approximate country or region, request date and time, requested hostname and URL path, and user-agent information—which may indicate your browser, operating system, device type, and, in some cases, device model—as well as HTTP response information, cache information, bandwidth usage, and other traffic-related technical information. We also process IP addresses and client identifiers to enforce rate limits, prevent abuse, and protect the Services. We use this information to deliver and secure the Services, troubleshoot errors, monitor performance, and understand general traffic patterns.

The information we collect includes:
* _Log and Usage Data._ Depending on the features you use, this information may include your IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, feature interactions, system activity, and crash dumps. If we introduce search or file-related features, it may also include searches performed and files viewed through the Services.
* _Location Data._ We derive an approximate country or region from your IP address. We do not collect GPS or precise device location. Because the Services are geographically restricted, concealing or blocking the information needed to determine your approximate location may prevent you from using the Services.

### Cookies and Similar Technologies
We use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to operate and secure the Services. These include cookies provided by Cloudflare for bot detection, security challenges, traffic management, and protection against malicious activity and a session cookie to maintain user authentication. These cookies are not used by us for advertising or cross-site behavioural tracking and cannot be disabled through the Services.

We also use browser local and session storage to retain theme, chat information, and login-state preferences and temporarily cache limited account information, model selections, and usage information. This storage is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

## 2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?
_**In Short:** We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We process the personal information for the following purposes listed below. We may also process your information for other purposes only with your prior explicit consent._

**We process your personal information for a variety of reasons, depending on how you interact with our Services, including:**
* **To facilitate account creation and authentication and otherwise manage user accounts.** We may process your information so you can create and log in to your account, as well as keep your account in working order.
* **To deliver and facilitate delivery of services to the user.** We may process your information to provide you with the requested service.

* **To respond to user inquiries/offer support to users.** We may process your information to respond to your inquiries and solve any potential issues you might have with the requested service.
* **To send administrative information to you.** We may process your information to send you details about our products and services, changes to our terms and policies, and other similar information.
* **To request feedback.** We may process your information when necessary to request feedback and to contact you about your use of our Services.
* **To protect our Services.** We may process your information as part of our efforts to keep our Services safe and secure, including fraud monitoring and prevention.
* **To evaluate and improve our Services and your experience.** We may process feedback, usage information, and service-performance information to identify usage trends, understand how our Services are used, and improve their features, reliability, safety, and user experience. We do not use conversation content for this purpose unless you have opted in as described below.
* **To comply with our legal obligations.** We may process your information to comply with our legal obligations, respond to legal requests, and exercise, establish, or defend our legal rights.
* **To evaluate, train, or improve CAI with your consent.** If you choose to opt in, we may process conversation content, feedback, and related usage information to evaluate, train, or improve CAI. You may withdraw your consent at any time.
* **To deliver and protect email communications.** We may process email-delivery, bounce, complaint, and suppression information to troubleshoot delivery problems and maintain our sender reputation.

If you enable model training, CAI's operator may manually review selected conversation content in its raw form to determine whether it is suitable for training. De-identified training examples may be stored on RunPod infrastructure and on operator-controlled local systems for evaluation, testing, and training of CAI-owned models.

## 9. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES
Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Policy.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
